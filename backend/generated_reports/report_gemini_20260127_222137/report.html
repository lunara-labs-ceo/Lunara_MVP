Executive Summary

This report provides an overview of sales performance, key trends, and customer demographics. Key findings include:

  • The Accessories category generates the highest revenue.
  • Average order value is slightly higher for male customers.
  • Order volume shows a clear upward trend throughout the year, peaking in December.

Revenue by Category

The bar chart above illustrates the total revenue generated by each product category. Accessories lead the way, indicating strong demand for these items.

Average Order Value by Gender

This chart compares the average order value between male and female customers. While both are relatively close, males tend to spend slightly more per order.

Monthly Order Trend

The line chart displays the trend of order counts over the months. There's a clear increase in order volume as the year progresses, with a significant spike in December, likely due to holiday shopping.
